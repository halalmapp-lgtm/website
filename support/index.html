Support

We’re here to help with the HalalMapp app and business tools.

Contact us

Email halalmapp@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can. Include your account email and a short description of the issue.

In the app

  • Settings → Support — email us from your phone
  • Settings → Report an issue — bugs, account help, or inappropriate content
  • Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account — permanently remove your account

Business subscriptions

Manage or cancel Pro / Advanced in the app under Plans & billing, or through the App Store / Google Play if you subscribed on mobile. Web subscriptions are managed from the business portal where you checked out.
