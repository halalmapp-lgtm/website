Delete your HalalMapp account

HalalMapp Inc.

In the app

  1. Open HalalMapp and tap the profile tab.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email developer@halalmapp.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". If you signed in with Apple's "Hide My Email", tell us your HalalMapp username instead. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we'll delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile, username and profile picture
  • Your phone number, friendships and contact matches
  • Your lists, rankings, visits, notes and photos, and passport stamps
  • Saved restaurants and saved or claimed deals
  • For restaurant accounts: listing photos, documents and certificates you uploaded

What we keep

Records the law requires us to keep or that we need to resolve disputes or prevent fraud, such as billing and tax records for business subscriptions, records of consent, and records about content removed for breaking the law. A paid subscription bought through Apple or Google must also be cancelled in your Apple or Google account, or it will keep renewing.

Delete some data but keep your account

You can delete individual visits, photos, lists and rankings in the app at any time. To have other data deleted, email us and say what you'd like removed. See our Privacy Policy for details.
