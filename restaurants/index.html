Founding Restaurant Program

What founding restaurants get

We are currently selecting a limited number of GTA restaurants for the initial pilot. Founding partners are not charged for the first six months.

  • Free access for 6 months

    Pilot restaurants receive complimentary HalalMapp business tools for six months. No payment is required to join the founding cohort.

  • Claim and customize your listing

    Take ownership of your restaurant page, update information, and add photos so diners see the right details.

  • Menu and hours where supported

    Keep hours, contact info, and menu details current from the business dashboard in the app.

  • Engagement analytics

    See how diners find, save, and interact with your listing from the existing business reports.

  • Coupons when you’re ready

    Create digital offers from the dashboard. Additional promotional tools are on the way — founding restaurants get early access.

  • Featured in HalalMapp social content

    Opportunity to be included in HalalMapp Instagram posts and stories as we introduce the app to GTA diners.

How it works

Three steps, then we handle the rest

  1. Tell us about your restaurant The form below takes about a minute. If your listing is already on HalalMapp, you can match it.
  2. We review and contact you The HalalMapp team reviews GTA fit and follows up by email or Instagram. This is a limited pilot, not an automatic signup.
  3. Claim your listing in the app You’ll create a business account and complete the existing verification flow. Founding restaurants are not asked to pay.
