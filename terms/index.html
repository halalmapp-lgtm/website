4. Halal information — please read

HalalMapp is a directory. We are not a halal certification body, and we do not inspect kitchens, suppliers or slaughter. Each listing carries one of these labels, set only by HalalMapp staff:

  • Hand slaughtered — the restaurant or its certifier states that its meat is hand-slaughtered according to Islamic guidelines.
  • Verified halal — we reviewed a certificate or documents stating the place is fully halal.
  • Halal options — some items are halal and others may not be. A place that serves alcohol is "Halal options" at most.
  • Unverified — we have not confirmed the listing's halal status.

Labels rely on information from restaurants, their certifiers, members' reports and public sources, which can change or be wrong. Where we show a certifier, it is the body the restaurant named. A label is not a guarantee. If halal matters to you, confirm with the restaurant before you order. Restaurants are responsible for what they serve and for the halal claims they make. Members' halal checks are their own experience and never change a label.
