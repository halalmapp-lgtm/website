Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Version 2026-10-30

This policy explains what personal information HalalMapp Inc. ("HalalMapp", "we") collects through the HalalMapp app and halalmapp.com, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

The short version

  • You can browse without an account. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Location, contacts, camera, photos and notifications are each optional and only used when you allow them.
  • Your visits, photos and notes are private unless you share them with friends or on a public list.
  • Photos are checked for harmful content by OpenAI before anyone else can see them, and searches you type are sent to OpenAI to understand them. You'll be told before either happens.
  • Our database is hosted in the United States.
  • You can download or delete your data in the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

HalalMapp Inc., 59 Glenview Avenue, Cambridge, Ontario N1R 2V7, Canada, is responsible for your personal information. Our Privacy Officer answers privacy questions and requests: developer@halalmapp.com.

2. Browsing without an account

If you browse without signing in, we don't create a profile for you. The area you're browsing and similar settings are stored only on your device. What you type into search is sent as described in section 5. If you ask us to launch in your city, we keep the city and the approximate location you gave, not who you are.

3. What we collect and why

  • Account: your email address, name or display name and username, and a profile picture if you add one, to create and run your account. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they share your name and email with us; with Apple's "Hide My Email" we receive only a relay address.
  • Phone number (Friends only): if you use Friends, the number you add, confirmed with a one-time code by text message, so that people who have your number can find you. Your number is never shown on your profile. You can stop people finding you this way on the Add friends screen.
  • Contacts (only if you choose): if you tap "Find friends from contacts" and allow access, the app sends the phone numbers in your address book to our servers once to see which belong to members. We keep only the matches (which members are in your contacts). We don't store the numbers of people who aren't members, we don't keep your contacts' names, and we never contact anyone in your address book.
  • Location (only if you allow it): while you use the app, to show restaurants and deals near you and give directions. When you check in at a restaurant for a passport stamp, your position is compared with the restaurant's and then discarded. We don't keep a history of where you've been.
  • What you save and log: saved restaurants and lists, rankings, visits (dishes, notes, photos and dates), passport stamps, and deals you save, claim and redeem.
  • Reports and support: halal-status reports (with any photo you attach), reports about other members, and messages you send us.
  • Notifications: a device token, so we can send the notifications you allow.
  • Records of your choices: which version of our Terms and this policy you agreed to and when, your notification settings, email unsubscribes, and your permission for photo checks.
  • If you run a restaurant: business and contact details, documents you upload to show you run the restaurant (which may include ID), halal certification details, and subscription and billing records. Card details are handled by Apple, Google or Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  • Technical information: our servers record basic request information, such as IP address and time, to keep the Service secure and working.

We don't infer your religion, health or other sensitive characteristics. We know that saving halal restaurants can say something about you, so we keep that activity private unless you choose to share it.

4. Who can see what you share

  • Your friends (people you've both accepted): your rankings, visits with dish photos and notes, and halal checks.
  • Anyone, including people without an account: lists you make public (new lists start as "Only me"), the visit photos and notes on a public list if you turn on "Include my photos and notes", and your Halal Passport if you share its link. A shared passport shows your first name and your totals and badges, never the names of restaurants.
  • Wherever you appear: your username, display name and profile picture.
  • Restaurants: counts of how many people viewed, saved, claimed and redeemed their deals, linked to anonymous account IDs. They don't see your name or contact details.

5. Search

When you search in your own words, such as "biryani open late under $15", the text you typed is sent to OpenAI to turn it into cuisines, dishes and filters. We send only those words, not your name, account or location. Please don't type personal information into search. If this isn't available, we search restaurant names instead.

6. Photo checks

Before a visit photo or profile picture can be seen by anyone else, it is sent to OpenAI's moderation service to check for sexual, graphically violent or self-harm content. We ask your permission the first time. Location and camera details are removed from the photo before it is uploaded. A photo that fails is deleted straight away and you're asked to choose another. We keep the result of each check (not the photo) so we can review mistakes. If you don't give permission, you can use HalalMapp without adding photos.

7. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run HalalMapp, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only for our purposes:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage. Hosted in the United States (Oregon).
  • OpenAI — search and photo checks (United States). Under OpenAI's API terms, this data isn't used to train its models and is kept for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • Google — maps and place information, Google sign-in, and push notifications through Firebase. Map use is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and App Store purchases.
  • RevenueCat, Stripe, Apple and Google Play — business subscriptions and payments.
  • Resend — email delivery.
  • Twilio — texting the one-time code that confirms your phone number (United States).

Because these providers are outside Canada, mainly in the United States, your information is stored and processed there and may be accessible to courts, law enforcement and national security authorities under those countries' laws.

We may also disclose information where the law requires or allows it, for example to respond to a court order, to report child sexual abuse material as Canadian law requires, or to protect someone's life, health or safety. If HalalMapp is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy, and we'll tell you.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, friends, lists, rankings, visits, photos and saved items: while your account exists, or until you delete them.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account and profile, friendships and contact matches, lists, rankings, visits, photos, profile picture and saved data straight away.
  • Business verification documents: while the business account exists, and deleted with it.
  • We keep some records after deletion where the law requires or where we need them to resolve disputes or prevent fraud, such as billing and tax records, records of consent, and records about content we removed for breaking the law.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy of your data: Settings → Account → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account, or see halalmapp.com/delete-account.
  • Correct your information: edit your profile, or email us.
  • Turn things off: location, contacts, camera, photos and notifications in your phone's settings; deal alerts in the app's Settings; being found by phone number on the Add friends screen; marketing email with the unsubscribe link. Turning one off doesn't stop the rest of the app working.
  • Ask about your information: email our Privacy Officer. We'll reply within 30 days, and tell you if we need longer.
  • Complain: contact our Privacy Officer first. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Children and teens

HalalMapp is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if we learn we have, we delete it. For everyone, including teens, sharing is off until you turn it on: new lists start as "Only me", location is used only if allowed, and we don't profile anyone for advertising.

11. Security

We encrypt information in transit, limit administrative tools to staff accounts, keep audit logs of sensitive changes, limit how often phone numbers and contacts can be looked up, and keep visit photos and business documents in private storage. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

12. Changes

We'll update this policy when our practices change. For material changes we'll tell you in the app before they apply. The date and version at the top show which policy is current.
